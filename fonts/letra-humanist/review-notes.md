These are the engine's own known weaknesses, so please don't stop at this list.

- **Construction:** letters are generated from one set of recipes and a pen model, so joins and curve tension may look systematic.
- **`g`:** the double-story form is the least resolved letter, with a fussy link and ear.
- **`m`:** there's a tiny notch where the arches meet the middle stem.
- **`&` and `@`:** these work, but are plainer than the rest of the font.
- **Figures:** default figures are proportional. Spacing comes from an automatic measurement and hasn't been tuned figure by figure.
- **Ligatures:** `fi` and `fl` are simply set side by side, so they aren't redrawn joined forms. In `fi`, the hook of `f` and the dot of `i` both stay.
- **Built letters:** `Æ`, `Œ`, `æ` and `œ` are assembled from other letters. `Œ` in particular may need redrawing.
- **Symbols:** `§`, `¶`, `®` and `™` are assembled or scaled from other shapes.
- **Kerning:** values are automatic, and only checked by eye in sample words.
- **Hinting:** autohinted only, and not yet seen on Windows or Linux.
- **Google Fonts:** the only failing check is the version number, because this is a pre-release below 1.000.
